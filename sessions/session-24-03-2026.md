## What we covered
Today was all about **Database Triggers**. These are automated scripts that act as event listeners. Unlike regular procedures that you run manually, these fire on their own whenever data changes.

* **Definition:** Special procedures attached to tables to keep data accurate or automate logs.
* **Timing:** Using **BEFORE** to check data before it saves, or **AFTER** to log changes once they are done.
* **Events:** Setting them to run on **INSERT**, **UPDATE**, or **DELETE**.
* **Values:** Using :old and :new to compare data from before and after a change.


## What I get
The best part is that they are invisible. You don't have to call them. You just update a row, and the trigger handles the rest. **BEFORE** is for validating data, while **AFTER** is for updating other tables or auditing.
